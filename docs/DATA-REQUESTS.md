# When someone asks about their data

For the owner. A family never uses the app, so a request reaches us through their mobed or by
email to the contact address. Reply within 30 days. Keep a short note of each request: the date,
what was asked, what was done. Do not keep their number in that note.

## 1. Check who is asking

Only erase for the person whose number it is, or a family member they have named.

Ask them to send the request **from that WhatsApp number**, or have their mobed confirm it. If you
cannot tell who is asking, do nothing yet and ask again. Erasing the wrong family cannot be undone.

## 2. Erase a family

In the app, as an admin: **Menu, Admin, Erase a family.**

1. Type the family's WhatsApp number, with its country code.
2. Tap **See what we hold**. It shows counts only.
3. Read them. If they look wrong, stop and find out why.
4. Tap **Erase for good**.

**What goes, from every mobed's book:** the family's name and number, their saved names, their
memorials and dates, the names recited at their ceremonies, the notes and location on their events,
and the record of messages sent to them.

**What stays:**
- A past or booked event stays on the mobed's calendar, marked "Erased". The slip still prints.
- If they replied STOP, that stays, so we go on honouring it.

**Each mobed is told** in the app that a family was erased, without saying which one. A booked
event for that family stays on their calendar with no name, so tell the mobed directly if the
family still expects them.

## 3. What the action cannot reach

- **Backups.** Nightly encrypted backups are kept for 30 days and pre-deploy copies for the last
  10 deploys. Their copy of the family ages out; nothing else needs doing. If a backup is ever
  restored within that time, run the erase again.
- **In-app notices** that mention the family expire within 7 days.
- **Meta.** WhatsApp keeps its own records of messages it delivered, under Meta's policy.

Say so in your reply.

## 4. A family asks what we hold

Use **See what we hold** for the counts. For the detail, ask the mobed who keeps the record: it is
private to them, and the admin screen does not show it (D2).

## 5. Reply

> We have removed your family's details from Mobed Diary. Our backups are deleted within 30 days,
> and your details go with them. If you replied STOP to our messages, we have kept that one record,
> so that you are not messaged again.

## 6. Old records

Nothing is deleted because of its age (DECISIONS.md D8). A record goes only when the mobed deletes
it, a mobed deletes their account, or a family asks and you erase them as in section 2.
