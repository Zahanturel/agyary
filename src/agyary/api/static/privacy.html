Privacy Policy

Mobed Diary · last updated 26 August 2026

Mobed Diary is a calendar and record-keeping tool for Zoroastrian priests (mobeds) managing ceremonies at a fire temple (agyari). This page explains exactly what it stores, who can see it, and how to get it removed.

This is an alpha. A small number of invited mobeds are using it while it is being built. Treat it accordingly: keep your own records, and do not put anything in it you could not afford to lose.

How signing in works

You are never asked to type a phone number. You tap Sign in with WhatsApp, we generate a one-time code, and you send that code to our WhatsApp Business number from your own WhatsApp. Your number reaches us inside the message that Meta delivers, cryptographically signed by Meta.

This matters for your privacy in two ways. Because nobody types a number, nobody can use this app to test whether a given number belongs to a mobed here. And because we only ever receive, we never send you a WhatsApp message of any kind: no codes, no marketing, no reminders over WhatsApp.

Meta sees that message, because it travels through WhatsApp. It is governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy, not this one. The message contains only a random sign-in code.

Record what the ceremony needs and no more.

What is never collected

  • No card, bank or UPI details. Where a ceremony has a fee, the app records an amount and whether it was paid. It never touches the payment itself and stores no payment instrument.
  • No location tracking. A ceremony can have a location because you typed one. Your device is never asked where it is.
  • No analytics, trackers, advertising or third-party scripts. The app loads nothing from anyone else's servers. Fonts are served from ours. Nothing here profiles you and nothing is sold or shared for marketing, ever.
  • No contact list access, no microphone, no camera, no photos.

Who else handles it

  • Meta (WhatsApp) carries your sign-in message, as described above.
  • Cloudflare terminates the HTTPS connection and passes traffic to the app.
  • The hosting provider running the virtual server the app and its database sit on.

Nobody else. There is no third party we hand this data to, and there is no arrangement under which anyone pays for access to it.

Retention

  • Sign-in attempts are deleted within minutes. They are swept automatically and carry no identity until claimed.
  • Ceremony records are kept for as long as the agyari uses the app. A diary is only useful with its history intact.
  • Your account is kept until you ask for it to be removed.

Security

Traffic is HTTPS end to end. The database is not reachable from the internet at all: it accepts connections only from the app, over a private network. Your session is a signed token that expires. Every message received from Meta has its signature verified before it is read, and one that cannot be verified is rejected. We are working to encrypt stored phone numbers at rest.

Getting your data, or getting it deleted

Write to turelzahan10@gmail.com from a mail account we can tie to your record, and say what you want: a copy of what is held about you, a correction, or deletion. Deletion of your account removes you and your preferences. Ceremony records that other mobeds at your agyari also rely on are handled case by case, and we will tell you plainly what can and cannot go.

If you are not a user of this app and believe a mobed has recorded your details here, write to the same address and say so. You do not need an account to ask.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected or who sees it, the date at the top changes and users are told in the app. Old versions are in the project's public git history.
