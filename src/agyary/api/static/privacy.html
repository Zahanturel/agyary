Privacy Policy

Mobed Diary · last updated 14 October 2026

Mobed Diary is a diary for Zoroastrian priests (mobeds): the ceremonies they perform at a fire temple, the families they serve, and the memorial prayers they keep for those families. This page says what it stores, who can see it, what it sends, and how to have it deleted.

Signing in

A mobed signs in with WhatsApp, in one of two ways: by sending a one-time code from their own WhatsApp to the Mobed Diary number (we learn their number from that message), or by typing their number and receiving a six digit code on WhatsApp. The number is used to sign them in and to keep their account. A sign-in is a session the mobed can end from the app at any time, on this phone or on all their others.

What a mobed records about families

A mobed keeps the details their work needs: the names and WhatsApp numbers of the families they serve, the names recited in prayers (living and departed), the dates and schedule of memorial prayers, and their ceremony records. These are private to the mobed who recorded them. Another mobed at the same fire temple cannot see them, even if they serve the same family.

Families do not use the app themselves. A mobed who records a family's details is expected to record only what the prayers need, and to tell the family.

WhatsApp reminders to families

  • A family is reminded only after their mobed has asked them and recorded that they agreed. The words the mobed reads out are kept with the agreement.
  • A reminder is one short message, the evening before a memorial prayer, from the Mobed Diary WhatsApp number. It is never sent during the first ten days after a death.
  • Replying STOP (or "Stop reminders", "Unsubscribe", "Cancel") stops all reminders to that number, from every mobed, for good. We send one short message to confirm, and nothing more.
  • Any other reply is passed to the family's mobed in the app.

Who else handles the data

  • Meta (WhatsApp) carries every message we send and receive. Its own privacy policy covers that.
  • Cloudflare sits in front of the website, for security and speed.
  • Oracle Cloud hosts the server and the database.

We do not sell data, show advertising, or use analytics or tracking of any kind.

Cookies and storage on your phone

One cookie keeps you signed in. The app also keeps its own screens on your phone so it opens quickly. There are no tracking cookies.

Keeping and deleting

  • Your data is kept while your account exists.
  • Delete your account from the app: Menu, then Delete my account. Your account and everything in it are deleted at once: your behdins and their saved names, your memorials and reminders, and the machis and bookings you made. See how to delete your account.
  • A family can ask their mobed to remove them, or write to us.
  • Copies in our backups are deleted as those backups are replaced.

Security

Everything travels over HTTPS. Each mobed sees only their own records. Sign-in sessions can be ended from the app, and an administrator can end all of a mobed's sessions if a phone is lost.

Children

Mobed Diary is for working priests. It is not meant for children.

Questions, or a request to see or delete data: privacy@gotiadarian.com.
