Mobed Diary

From the app

  1. Open Mobed Diary and sign in.
  2. Open the Menu.
  3. Tap Delete my account, then Delete everything.

Your account is deleted at once, with everything in it: your behdins and their saved names, your memorials and reminders, the machis and bookings you made, and your sign-in sessions. It cannot be undone. Copies in our backups are deleted as those backups are replaced.

If you cannot sign in

Write to contact@gotiadarian.com from any address, with the WhatsApp number of the account. We will confirm it is yours on that number before deleting anything, and reply when it is done.

Families

If a mobed has recorded your family's details, you can ask them to remove you, or write to the address above. To stop WhatsApp reminders, reply STOP to any reminder.
